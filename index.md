I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Peking University, advised by Prof. Sujian Li. I am currently a research intern at Tencent, working on LLM post-training.

I want to understand how large language models actually work, and use that understanding to make them learn more efficiently, safer and more reliable.

My research centers on these questions:

- **Robustness and interpretability:** what internal mechanisms make LLMs fail?
- **Efficient learning:** what data, and what training signals, make models better and learn more efficiently?
